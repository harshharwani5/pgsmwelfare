Consumer Psychology Insights

Commitment Phobia is real for urban tech workers. By highlighting the short 11-month lease term against a long-term furniture purchase, we evoke the feeling of "buying baggage." The visual of a high-end emerald green sofa shows that renting isn't cheap-looking—it's an aspirational lifestyle upgrade that matches their income and ambition without the burden of ownership.
