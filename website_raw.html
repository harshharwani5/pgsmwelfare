Who We Are?

MapInn Foundation is a non-profit social organization working towards education, health, women empowerment, and sustainable community development. Founded by Mr. Mahendra Prasad Sharma, the Foundation is part of a broader vision to unite NGOs under one umbrella — the Society of NGO Awareness (SONA) — connecting over 275 NGOs across 52 districts of Madhya Pradesh.
Driven by compassion and purpose, MapInn Foundation partners with individuals, corporates, and government bodies to create long-term impact through awareness, training, and rural development initiatives.

Our Vision

To build a compassionate, educated, and self-reliant India where every individual has access to opportunities, equality, and dignity.

Inspired by the values of Mother Teresa, Shri Kailash Satyarthi, and Dr. S.N. Subbarao, we follow the path of humanity and service to create a just and inclusive society.

Our Mission

Our mission is to serve humanity through action and awareness:

  • Promote education and skill development for underprivileged communities.
  • Support women empowerment and entrepreneurship.
  • Organize health camps, environment drives, and awareness programs.
  • Provide relief and support during natural disasters and pandemics.
  • Collaborate with corporates and government departments under CSR programs.

Our Work & Initiatives

During natural disasters and pandemics, MapInn Foundation has actively supported affected families through food distribution, essential supply drives, and community rebuilding efforts. Our volunteers ensure no one is left behind

Standing together in times of crisis

1. Education for All
Empowering young minds through scholarships, school support, and digital literacy campaigns.
2. Women Empowerment
Providing training and self-employment opportunities to women in rural and semi-urban areas.
3. Health & Nutrition
Organizing free medical camps, blood donation drives, and spreading awareness on hygiene and nutrition.
4. Environment & Agriculture
Promoting organic farming, tree plantation, and sustainable agricultural practices with local farmers.
5. Relief & Rehabilitation
